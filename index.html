Watson Personal Finance
A personal budgeting app

One person's money, kept on one computer

Watson Personal Finance is a budgeting app built and used by one person to keep track of their own accounts. It isn't offered to the public: there are no sign-ups and no other users.

One userOnly its developer uses it, for their own accounts.
Stays at homeData is kept only on the developer's own computer, encrypted.
Never shared or soldNo ads, no analytics, no data brokers.

What it does

  • Shows balances for checking, savings, credit card, loan and investment accounts in one place.
  • Sorts transactions into categories and tracks spending against a monthly budget.
  • Lists upcoming bills and card payments, and tracks net worth and investments over time.

How it connects to banks

The app connects to banks through Plaid. The developer signs in to their bank inside Plaid's own window and chooses which accounts to share; the app never sees bank usernames or passwords. It reads balances, transactions, credit card and loan details, and investment holdings, and only uses them to show the developer their own finances. Any bank can be disconnected at any time, which ends the app's access straight away.
